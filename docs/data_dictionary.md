# Primary predictor dictionary

The primary specification used **17 harmonised raw predictors** organised into four analytical domains.

> The domain structure was used for organisation, ablation and SHAP aggregation. It was **not** a separate fusion architecture.

## Demographic

1. **Age** — respondent age in years.
2. **Sex** — harmonised women/men indicator from the sex-specific recode source.

## Structural and socioeconomic

3. **Residence** — urban/rural residence.
4. **Region** — Zambia region/province category as represented in the recode.
5. **Education** — harmonised education category.
6. **Wealth** — DHS household wealth quintile.

## Behavioural and relationship

7. **Marital status** — harmonised current union/marital status.
8. **Age at first sexual intercourse** — numeric age after codebook-compliant handling of special values.
9. **Sexual-debut status** — categorical companion variable distinguishing substantive non-age states such as never had sex / inconsistent response from numeric age.
10. **Condom use at most recent sexual episode** — recent protective-behaviour indicator.
11. **Reported STI in the previous 12 months** — recent self-reported infection/symptom indicator.
12. **Number of partners in the previous 12 months** — recent partner exposure.
13. **Lifetime number of partners** — cumulative partner exposure.
14. **Male circumcision status** — sex-specific factor with explicit female not-applicable representation in the harmonised dataset.

## Health and testing history

15. **Ever tested for HIV** — prior contact with HIV testing services.
16. **Months since most recent HIV test** — recency of prior testing engagement.
17. **Received most recent HIV test result** — completion of the prior testing encounter.

## Outcome

**HIV biomarker status** was derived from valid binary `HIV03` laboratory categories in the linked HIV biomarker recode. The outcome is contemporaneous biomarker status, not future acquisition risk or a clinical diagnosis generated by the model.

## Important coding note

DHS special values were interpreted according to Zambia DHS documentation. In particular, special codes for age at first sex were not treated as literal ages. Numeric and status representations were separated before modelling.
