# AI Biology and Medicine submission requirements

Verified 29 September 2026 in the official [Guide for authors](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/ai-biology-and-medicine/publish/guide-for-authors), accessed through the browser after automated retrieval failed.

For a Research Article: up to 4,000 main-text words excluding abstract, methods, references and legends; an unstructured abstract up to 250 words; at most eight tables/figures; approximately 50 references. Use an unheaded introduction, Results, Discussion and Methods. Results and Methods may have topical subsections; Discussion should not.

Supply editable single-column Word text, editable tables and separate figure files. References use numbered square brackets in citation order. Optional highlights require three to five points of at most 85 characters each. Include data availability, funding, competing interests, CRediT contributions and a declaration of generative-AI assistance. Verify the actual institutional ethics determination and consent documentation; do not invent approval details. The guide currently states an APC waiver for submissions before 31 December 2028.

**Editorial fit remains a substantial risk.** The journal gives priority to computational and experimental advances; applications of existing methods without experimental follow-up require unusually consequential findings. Our assessment is that correcting this study improves credibility but does not by itself establish the significance needed for acceptance. The manuscript should emphasise its substantive eligibility and subgroup findings rather than advertise standard XGBoost as a new algorithm.
