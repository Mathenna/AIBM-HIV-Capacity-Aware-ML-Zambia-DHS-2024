# Data access

The underlying individual-level data are from the **Zambia Demographic and Health Survey 2024 (ZDHS 2024)**.

The authors obtained authorised access through The DHS Program. The study uses women's individual recode (IR), men's individual recode (MR), and HIV biomarker recode (AR) data.

## Redistribution restriction

This repository does **not** redistribute DHS microdata, respondent-level extracts, DHS recode files, or related distribution files. Researchers who wish to reproduce the analysis must request access directly from The DHS Program and comply with the applicable DHS terms of use.

Files that must not be committed here include, among others:

- `.dta` / `.DTA`
- `.sav` / `.SAV`
- `.DCT` / `.dct`
- `.DO` / `.do`
- `.frq` / `.frw`
- `.map` / `.MAP`
- other raw or respondent-level DHS source files

## Approved access used for this study

The lead author reported that access to Zambia DHS 2024 data was requested through her own DHS Program account and approved on approximately **17 March 2026**.

No credentials, authorisation emails, or personal account details are stored in this repository.
